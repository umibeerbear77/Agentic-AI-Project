****Leaving Policy****
	The leaving policy of the company has three types that the employees have to follow from these rules. The first one is leaving in advance. The workers who want it must inform at least 14 days in advance at least 14 days and for this policy, the employee can not leave for more than 7 days. Also, leaving in advance can not be used more than 3 times in 1 year, the leaving in advance has to be approved by your leader in your department only. Second is vacation leave, which must be requested at least 30 days but no more than 366 days in advance. Any request submitted less than 30 days in advance will not be approved. Also, employees cannot take more than 30 days of vacation leave, and it must be approved by the head of your department only. However, vacation leave can be taken no more than once a year. The third is leaving from accident, such as sick (fever, diarrhea, rash, etc.), an accident from vehicle that if it happens, it will cause you critical damage or property damage, flooding and including with the others accident that causes you to leave in suddenly because of these cases, so the workers who faced with these cases can leave by do not tell in advance, leaving from accident can leave not more than 3 days, and it is counted from the day that the accident happened but if you leave more than 3 days the employees have to bring medical certificate or damage report or the other documents that prove your sincerity to show with your principal in the day that you can come to the company for sign to certify but if that day your boss is not in the company you can bring your evidences to another principal in the other department to sign, the leaving from accident has no limit to leave.
Note: 1 year = 366 days

	The workers who want to change their career paths, you can follow the 2 rules as follows: The first one transfers the career path in your department you must have worked in or hold a position not less much than 2 years, and you have to check with the history work that it looks satisfy satisfies or not. 	The second choice for changing career paths is changing career paths with other departments.
